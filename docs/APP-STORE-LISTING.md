## Description (max 4000)

```
Nithyakarma helps you keep up your daily anushtanams - Sandhyavandhanam, japam and parayanams - and see at a glance what is done today and what is left.

SANDHYAVANDHANAM, EVERY DAY
Mark Prathakala, Madhyanika and Saayamkala sandhya as you finish them. Missed yesterday's? You can still mark it the next day, so one late evening does not break your practice.

TRACK WHAT YOUR HOUSEHOLD PRACTISES
Vishnu Sahasranamam, Lalitha Sahasranamam, Hanuman Chalisa, Narayaneeyam, Bhagavad Gita, Aditya Hrudayam, Sri Rudram, Devi Mahatmyam, Soundarya Lahari and more - daily, weekly, count-based, or read in order with your chapter progress kept for you.

A STREAK THAT RESPECTS A MISSED DAY
Earn punya as you practise and climb from Shishya to Brahmarishi. Streak freezes, earned as you go, keep a single missed day from erasing months of practice.

REMINDERS AT THE RIGHT TIME
Gentle reminders at your sandhya times and in the evening if the day is not done yet, following your own time zone.

LEARN AND READ
Read stotrams verse by verse, follow the Ramayanam kandam by kandam, and open the Devi Mahatmyam and other texts right inside the app.

TODAY'S PANCHANGAM AND CALENDAR
Thithi, nakshatram and Rahu kalam, Yamagandam and Gulika kalam on your daily screen, and a calendar marking amavasya, tharpanam days and festivals, in the Tamil or Kerala tradition.

YOUR CHILDREN'S PRACTICE TOO
Add family members under 15 and mark their anushtanams for them. Each child gets their own streak. Children never sign in; you manage everything.

AN OPTIONAL COMMUNITY
Turn on Sabha any time to compare streaks and punya with others. It is off by default, and you choose whether your name appears.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
Sign in with Apple or email. Delete your account and everything in it, any time, from inside the app. We never sell your data.

Nithyakarma is free. Built for the daily Sandhyavandhanam and parayanam tradition, and open to anyone keeping up a daily practice.
```

### Notes (max 4000)

```
Thank you for reviewing Nithyakarma.

WHAT THE APP IS
A daily tracker for Hindu religious practices (anushtanams) such as Sandhyavandhanam, japam and parayanam (reading of scripture). Users mark what they have done each day, keep a streak, earn points ("punya"), get reminders, read traditional texts and see the traditional calendar (panchangam).

DEMO ACCOUNT
The account above is set up as an adult male user with Sandhyavandhanam and other practices, one family member, and today's Sandhya already marked.
- Today: tap a practice to mark it done. Sandhyavandhanam has three times of day; any one completes the day.
- Learning: stotrams verse by verse, the Ramayanam by kandam, and other texts.
- Calendar: the traditional monthly calendar with festival and tharpanam days.
- Profile: family members, reminder settings, community settings, and account deletion.

WHY AN ACCOUNT IS NEEDED
Everything in the app is personal and kept across devices: daily logs, streaks, points, family members' progress and reminder times (sent as push notifications at the user's own sandhya times). That is why the app asks users to sign in. Sign in with Apple is offered alongside email.

NATIVE FEATURES
Push notification reminders, Sign in with Apple, the iOS share sheet for inviting family, haptic feedback, and the App Store rating prompt. The app's interface is bundled in the app, not loaded from a website.

SANDHYAVANDHANAM ELIGIBILITY
By tradition Sandhyavandhanam is performed by men (and boys after upanayanam), so the app offers it only to male profiles. Women's profiles get the other practices.

COMMUNITY (SABHA)
An optional leaderboard, off by default. It shows only a display name, tier and streak; for children only a first name, and only if the parent opts them in. There are no posts, messages, photos or comments. Users can hide themselves any time (Profile > Community). On the leaderboard, the "..." button next to any other name lets a user report it (it reaches us and we act on reports promptly) or hide it from their own view. Reports can also be sent to support@nithyakarma.org.

ADS AND PURCHASES
This version shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Profile > Danger zone > type your email > Delete my account. This deletes the account and all its data. For Sign in with Apple users the app also revokes its Apple ID access.

CHILDREN
Parents can add children under 15 as family members and mark practices for them. Children never sign in, and the app is not aimed at children (not in the Kids category).
```
