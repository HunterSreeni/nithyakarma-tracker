Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Nithyakarma

This policy explains what Nithyakarma collects in the app and how it is used. The app is a personal tracker for your Hindu, Brahmin-tradition daily practices; we collect only what is needed to run it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email and password (handled by our auth provider; the password is stored only as a secure hash), or, if you use Sign in with Apple on iPhone, the email Apple shares with us (which can be Apple's private relay address) and your Apple user ID.
  • Profile: display name and gender (used only to offer gender-appropriate practices such as Sandhyavandhanam), plus your streaks, punya points, tier and referral code.
  • Family members you add: name, gender, upanayanam status and opt-in flags.
  • Practice data: which anushtanams you track, your daily logs, and your timezone (read from your device's clock setting, not your location) so reminders and your daily streak follow your own day rather than a server's.
  • Notifications: your reminder preference and push tokens for web, Android and iPhone.
  • Birth details (optional): if you open My birth chart, the date, time and place of birth you enter are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us, to our analytics or to anyone else, and you can delete them on that screen. On Android they are not included in device backups. On iPhone they can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup of the phone, which we cannot access. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Advertising (Android and iPhone): the only ad is an optional short video you can choose to watch for an extra birth chart download. When it plays, Google AdMob uses device identifiers and an approximate location worked out from your IP address to serve and measure it. On Android this can include the advertising ID; on iPhone we never ask for tracking permission, so iOS does not share the advertising ID with AdMob. Ad-free users never see it.

How we use it

To provide the tracker, compute streaks and tiers, send the reminders you enable, show community leaderboards (which you can opt out of), and apply referrals.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase - authentication and database hosting.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) - delivering push reminders on Android and iPhone (on iPhone through Apple Push Notification service).
  • Apple - Sign in with Apple on iPhone. When you delete your account we also ask Apple to revoke Nithyakarma's access to your Apple ID.
  • Google AdMob - the optional rewarded video on Android and iPhone (skipped when ad-free); AdMob may use device identifiers and approximate location per Google's policies. On first use we ask for consent where required (EEA/UK), and you can review or change that choice.
  • Sentry - anonymous crash, error and performance reports to keep the app stable (no IP addresses or personal data).
  • PostHog (EU servers) - product analytics: which screens are visited, how people found us (campaign tags) and whether key steps happen, for example a practice being marked. Events are tied to a random account ID (the same pseudonymous ID our database uses), never your name or email, and never include which practices you do or your gender.
  • WhatsApp - the "share" button simply opens a WhatsApp link with text you choose; we do not send your data to WhatsApp ourselves.

Leaderboards

If you appear on a leaderboard, other members can see your display name, tier and streak. You can hide yourself any time from Profile → Community. Children's entries (Bala Sabha) show first name only and require parent opt-in.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account any time from Profile → Danger zone. This permanently removes your profile, family-member profiles, all logs, streaks and leaderboard entries. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Nithyakarma's access to your Apple ID.

Security & children

Passwords are hashed and row-level security restricts your data to you. Family-member profiles are managed entirely by the parent/guardian - children do not log in and we do not collect data directly from them.

Web analytics (nithyakarma.org & app.nithyakarma.org)

The marketing site at nithyakarma.org uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and a small cookieless PostHog counter for visit counts and campaign tags. Neither uses cookies or collects personal information; the counter keeps a random visit ID only until you close the browser tab. In the app (web, Android and iPhone) we use PostHog, hosted in the EU, to understand how people find and use Nithyakarma. Events carry a random account ID and simple facts such as "a practice was marked" or "the day was completed", never your name, email, gender or which practices you do. PostHog uses your IP address once, when an event arrives, to work out your approximate location (country, region and city) and then discards the IP address; only that approximate location is kept. On Android we read the Google Play install referrer once, to learn which campaign link led to the install. Once you delete your account, that random ID no longer links to you.
